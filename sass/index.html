Welcome to Sticky Im's

🇹🇭 From Kalasin to Your Table


My name is Immily and I'm originally from Kalasin (กาฬสินธุ์) in northeastern Thailand.


Growing up, food was always at the centre of family life. The recipes I cook today have been lovingly passed down through generations.


Every dish at Sticky Im's is made using authentic Thai ingredients and traditional family recipes.


I only prepare small batches every weekend, so every serving is freshly made with care.
